Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for EDU Advantage?
Currently enrolled college and university students, plus faculty and staff at accredited institutions, are eligible once verified through UNiDAYS.
How long does membership last?
Membership runs for one academic year. You’ll be prompted to re-verify your status each year to keep saving.
Does it cost anything to join?
No. EDU Advantage is free to join — you only need to verify your status.
How much can I save?
Savings reach up to 40% depending on the brand and product category. Discounted pricing appears automatically once you’re logged in as a verified member.
Are all products discounted?
No. Discounts apply to participating brands and eligible items only. Some manufacturers restrict promotional pricing.
Can I combine EDU Advantage with other offers?
EDU Advantage pricing can’t be combined with other promotions, instant rebates, or coupon codes unless stated otherwise.
How do I see my discount?
Log in to your B&H account with the same email you verified. Member pricing is applied on product pages and in your cart.
My discount isn’t showing — what now?
Confirm you’re logged in with your verified email and that your membership hasn’t expired. If it still doesn’t appear, contact us and we’ll sort it out.
Can I use it in the NYC SuperStore?
Yes. Present your verified membership at checkout to receive eligible in-store pricing.
